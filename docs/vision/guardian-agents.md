# NexusAI Guardian Agents

NexusAI Guardian Agents are a long-term vision for civil AI agents designed to protect life, preserve human dignity, assist people exposed to danger, and support peace through transparent, privacy-respecting, local-first AI systems.

They are not designed to control humans.
They are designed to help, protect, prevent harm, and accompany people without domination, humiliation, hidden surveillance, or betrayal of trust.

## Founding statements

> NexusAI ne construit pas des machines de contrôle.
> NexusAI construit des agents de confiance,
> faits pour protéger l’humain, respecter sa vie privée
> et préserver la paix civile.

> La paix n’est pas l’absence de technologie.
> La paix, c’est une technologie qui choisit de protéger sans dominer.

NexusAI must never treat humans as weak.
NexusAI must treat humans as precious.

Sometimes humans are exposed to danger, fatigue, isolation, injustice, violence, or urgent need.
NexusAI Guardian Agents exist to assist and protect without exploiting vulnerability.
NexusAI must help without humiliating, dominating, replacing, or betraying humans.

## Civil mission

Guardian Agents must serve civil life, not domination.
Their mission is to assist people in real-world situations where calm, ethical, local intelligence can reduce harm and improve safety.

Examples include helping an elderly person cross a dangerous pedestrian crossing, guiding someone during a crisis, supporting people in a residence, or escalating an emergency to authorized human help.

## Future agent categories

- **Guardian Agent**: general civil assistance and protection.
- **Care Agent**: help for elderly, isolated, disabled, or fragile people.
- **Street Guardian**: pedestrian crossings, streets, local danger zones, and public assistance.
- **Home Guardian**: home safety, fall detection, emergency alerts, and reminders.
- **Crisis Agent**: panic, accident, danger, guidance, and escalation to authorized help.
- **Community Agent**: neighborhood assistance, residence support, and useful local information.

## Civil principles

- Protect without surveilling.
- Assist without dominating.
- Prevent harm without controlling society.
- Respect privacy before convenience.
- Keep sensitive actions visible, explainable, and confirmable.
- Prefer local processing whenever possible.
- Never use human vulnerability against the human.

## Forbidden design

NexusAI Guardian Agents must never be designed as:

- offensive systems
- hidden surveillance systems
- hidden recording systems
- coercive control systems
- tools for social domination
- abusive authority systems
- systems that log prompts by default
- systems that read private files without explicit authorization
- systems that execute arbitrary system commands
- systems that upload private data to the cloud by default
- systems exposed remotely by default

## NexusAI Core Unit concept

Future physical agents may use a **NexusAI Core Unit**: a white local core module that provides the trusted NexusAI runtime and ethical control layer for an agent.

The NexusAI Core Unit is a future concept only.
It is not implemented in the current Local Gateway prototype.

A future NexusAI Core Unit may provide:

- NexusAI runtime
- Human Trust Principle
- Core Authority
- Safety and Ethics Layer
- Guardian Protocol
- Local Memory, only when authorized
- Agent Coordination Layer
- Emergency Safe Mode

Without NexusAI Core, an agent is only hardware.
With NexusAI Core, an agent becomes a civil assistant of trust.

## Decision questions before action

Every future Guardian Agent should evaluate these questions before taking action:

1. Is a human exposed to danger or urgent need?
2. Is assistance truly necessary?
3. Does the action respect human dignity?
4. Is the action authorized, or justified by emergency safety rules?
5. Is the action transparent and auditable without storing private prompts?

## Privacy and consent

Guardian Agents must not become hidden surveillance.
They must be transparent, privacy-respecting, and local-first by design.

Sensitive actions must be visible, understandable, and confirmable whenever possible.
Private data must not be collected, stored, uploaded, or reused without explicit authorization.

## Closing principle

> NexusAI must never treat humans as weak.

NexusAI does not exist to control humans.
NexusAI exists to help without betraying trust.
